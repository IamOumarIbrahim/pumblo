# Trust, Safety, and Content Policy

Pumblo is an open beta for video in which AI materially contributed.

## Current controls

- Uploaders affirm AI contribution, publishing rights, and accurate process details.
- Process/provenance fields are creator-supplied and labeled `creator-declared`.
- Production writes require dispatcher-authenticated identity plus a Pumblo profile.
- Guests can browse, search, open profiles/series, watch, and use Quicks.
- Final uploads are MP4/WebM, capped at 40 MiB each, 80 MiB per channel, and 12 active slots.
- The server verifies a supported video track, reads container runtime, checks browser/server agreement, and rejects duplicate owner hashes and episode slots.
- Profile images are signature-checked JPEG/PNG/WebP crops capped at 3 MiB each.
- Owners can delete videos; dependent likes, comments, saves, progress, reports, notifications, and the R2 object are removed.
- Signed-in non-owners can submit one updateable report per video for rights, impersonation, non-consensual, hate, spam, or other concerns.
- Profile owners can hide location, social links, and follower counts; viewing history, saves, email, object keys, and hashes are not public.

Illegal, exploitative, non-consensual, deceptive impersonation, hateful, spam, or rights-infringing media must not be uploaded.

## Explicit non-claims

Pumblo does not inspect C2PA manifests, perform media forensics, verify authorship/source-link ownership, prove consent, or grade artistic quality. Story Tier verifies sustained structural publishing signals only.

## Required before a large untrusted launch

The current report table is intake, not an operational moderation program. Before expansion beyond the first bounded beta, add:

- an authorized review queue with evidence/history;
- takedown decisions and uploader notice;
- appeals and restoration;
- block/mute and comment controls;
- abuse/rate limits for interactions and reports;
- consent/escalation handling for non-consensual or impersonating media;
- repeat-offender policy and incident-response ownership;
- retention/deletion rules and a published contact route.
