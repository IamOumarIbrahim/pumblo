# Trust, Safety, and Content Policy

Pumblo is an open beta for video in which AI materially contributed.

- The uploader must affirm AI contribution, publishing rights, and the accuracy of supplied process details.
- Current process/provenance information is creator-supplied and visibly labeled `creator-declared`.
- Pumblo does not inspect C2PA manifests, perform media forensics, verify human authorship, or grade artistic quality.
- Production writes require an authenticated person with a Pumblo profile.
- Public browsing, search, profiles, playback, and Quicks do not require authentication.
- Each creator is limited to two active MP4/WebM videos of at most 40 MiB each.
- Each profile image is restricted to a signature-checked JPEG/PNG/WebP crop of at most 3 MiB.
- Owners can permanently delete a video and its associated likes/comments.
- Illegal, exploitative, non-consensual, impersonating, or rights-infringing media must not be uploaded.

The application does not yet include user reporting, removal appeals, media moderation, abuse rate limits, consent handling, block/mute tools, or incident response. Those controls are release blockers before promotion to a large, untrusted public community.
