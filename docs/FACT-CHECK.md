# Fact Check and Positioning Notes

Checked July 31, 2026. Product language is intentionally narrower than the available evidence.

## Positioning

Pumblo is not another generation model and does not claim to replace one. It is a tool-neutral, creator-owned home after the render: AI-only public video, connected stories, audience interaction, creator identity, portable URLs, explicit credits, and transparent capacity limits.

This positioning responds to a real creator risk: work and audiences should not depend on a single generation vendor's gallery, pricing label, or account policy. Pumblo does not repeat allegations about any named vendor as product claims; it turns that motivation into independently testable design choices.

“Behind the render” stays secondary. Process metadata supports the video-first product.

## What Pumblo verifies

- identity for writes comes from the Sites Sign in with ChatGPT dispatcher;
- stored file size matches the declared/final upload size;
- MP4/WebM contains a supported video track;
- runtime is read from the stored media container and agrees with the browser preview;
- a creator cannot reuse the exact same file hash or episode slot;
- publication time is generated by the server;
- likes, comments, follows, progress, saves, notifications, and reports are persisted.

## What Pumblo does not verify

The [C2PA 2.3 explainer](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.3/explainer/_attachments/Explainer.pdf) distinguishes tamper-evident provenance evidence from a truth judgment. Pumblo does not inspect C2PA manifests or perform media forensics.

It therefore cannot prove that:

- a video is forensically AI-generated;
- every element was made by the uploader;
- a depicted scene is true or consensual;
- creator links are ownership-verified or endorsements;
- a series has a coherent plot;
- Story Tier represents artistic quality, originality, identity, or popularity.

AI/process details remain visibly `creator-declared`.

## Story Tier claim

Story Tier is a structural publishing signal. C/B/A thresholds use only owned series, consecutive episode numbering, server-read runtime, server timestamps, and countable series/episode totals. Exact duplicate files and episode slots are blocked. The rules are public and unit-tested.

That reduces easy abuse but does not eliminate low-effort distinct files, collusion, or meaningless episodic labeling. It must never be marketed as verified creative quality.

## Compression claim

Pumblo offers optional browser-side WebM re-encoding. It is “conservative” because the result is discarded unless estimated savings exceed 8% and runtime remains within tolerance. Final runtime and track validity are independently checked by the server.

It is not lossless, invisible under all content conditions, faster than real time, universally browser-supported, or a server transcoding service. Storage savings are local estimates and do not affect ranking, quota math, or Story Tier.

## Hosting and search

The 8,600 MiB modeled media envelope is below direct R2's currently published 10 GB-month Standard-storage free allowance. Direct allowances do not establish Sites-managed quotas. Pumblo claims a currently working no-card Sites path and a bounded 100-creator design—not free hosting forever or concurrent-load proof.

Canonical URLs, structured metadata, robots rules, and a sitemap make the domain crawlable and queryable. Search-engine indexing and ranking are controlled by search engines and are not guaranteed.

## Safety and analytics

Pumblo now persists one report per viewer/video and exposes a private activity inbox and Creator Studio. Reporting is intake, not a full moderation operation. Studio shows totals and per-video counts, not retention curves, attribution, audience demographics, revenue, or fraud detection.

## Dependency security

`npm audit --omit=dev` remains a required release gate for deployed dependencies. Development-only advisories are documented rather than hidden by forcing incompatible toolchain upgrades.
